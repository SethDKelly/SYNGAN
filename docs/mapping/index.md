## Core mapping rules

> **Inspection exposes owned or validly derived truth; it does not create a second owner.**

> **Words may simplify presentation, but may not erase ownership, semantic dimension, historical scope, uncertainty or disclosure meaning.**

> **Physical interaction may adapt to the host, but may not redefine SYNGAN semantics.**

> **Concept inclusion defines available capability; it does not require every included concept to be re-executed in every invocation.**

> **Human/programmatic parity requires equivalent material semantics for the same authorized context, not identical ergonomics.**

## Product form

Primary interaction remains package/SDK, notebook and embedded automation. CLI, reports, rich presentation, network service exposure and dedicated operator/admin applications remain optional adapters/integrations.
