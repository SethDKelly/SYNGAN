## Integrity decision

Phase 010 introduces no new concept, synchronization, universal inclusion edge or mandatory application-family edge.

It preserves:

- direct versus learned-state-assisted Generation;
- semantic versus operational completion;
- candidate versus authoritative output;
- Evaluation versus Evidence;
- Evidence versus approval/release/privacy guarantee;
- Provenance relationship versus source-fact authority;
- current versus exact historical truth;
- capability-specific degradation;
- authorization-relative disclosure;
- enterprise-scale bounded inspection.

No Workflow, global Status, Actionability, Recovery, Degraded Mode, History Quality, Disclosure State, Topology, Text, Platform Job, Dashboard, Result or Approval concept is justified by the mapping work.
