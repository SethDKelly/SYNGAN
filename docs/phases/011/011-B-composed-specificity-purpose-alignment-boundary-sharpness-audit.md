# 011-B — Composed Specificity, Purpose Alignment & Boundary Sharpness Audit

Apply the 011-A validation method to methodology obligation G1 and determine whether every accepted concept remains specifically justified and sharply bounded when SYNGAN is evaluated as a complete mapped application family rather than as eleven isolated specifications.

## Principal findings

All eleven concepts retain distinct current purposes in composition.

Particularly important results:

- Data Meaning remains descriptive authority rather than Constraint/metadata/schema/Relationship;
- Synthesis Strategy remains reusable synthesis-behavior authority rather than plugin/runtime/configuration infrastructure;
- Learning and Learned State remain a legitimate activity/result split despite mutual inclusion;
- Generation remains independently coherent in the direct `G-KERNEL` and is not a coordinator over the full suite;
- Constraint remains distinct from Data Meaning, Generation Condition and Evaluation Criterion;
- Evaluation Criterion → Evaluation → Evidence remains a question/examination/finding decomposition;
- Execution remains operational realization and gains specificity from its one-of domain-activity prerequisite rather than needing standalone scheduler purpose;
- Provenance remains typed historical relationship authority with high fan-in but deliberately low authority fan-out.
