No candidate now meets the promotion burden. Important current dispositions include:

- Generation Request and Condition remain Generation-owned;
- Attempt and checkpoint/recovery state remain Execution-owned/subordinate;
- Artifact/Dataset identity remains representation/integration;
- reproducibility remains a cross-cutting contract;
- Relationship remains descriptive structural state in Data Meaning despite current O15 scope;
- generic Privacy remains rejected, while future composable DP retains a mechanism-specific rediscovery trigger;
- Use/Release Decision remains external authority;
- Resource/Admission/Backpressure/Approximation/DegradedMode/Cost remain owner-specific, cross-cutting or deployment policy;
- Synthetic Output remains Generation-owned result state because current scope supplies no independent output lifecycle;
- Source/Dependency/Authorization/Secret/Platform Capability remain referenced context, security authority or architecture rather than synthetic-data concepts;
- topology/text vocabulary and familiar umbrella terms do not become concepts merely because they may appear in interfaces/implementation.

008-G closes B1, B2 and B5 and completes C8 across the catalog perimeter. The accepted catalog remains eleven concepts and fifteen synchronizations with no restore/add/remove/merge/split/rename.
