## Core parity finding

Human/programmatic parity means equivalent material semantics, not identical ergonomics.

For the same authorized context and conceptual question/action, surfaces must preserve the same material:

```text
concept owner
semantic state / current-use state
contextual assessment / actionability
operational Execution / Attempt state when present
candidate/finality distinction
exact bindings / historical orientation
Evidence finding / claim strength / limitations
Constraint applicability/handling distinction
disclosure state
historical-knowledge quality
authority-continuity qualification
safe next-action semantics
```

Notebook/human interaction may summarize/explain. Automation may expose structured bounded facts. Neither may establish stronger or different domain truth.

Parity is authorization-relative: different authorized actors may legitimately receive different outward detail, including non-disclosing responses when existence itself is protected.

## Recovery / authority-continuity result

A potentially regressive restore cannot be presented as ordinary current authority.

Human and programmatic interaction can both preserve the equivalent of:

```text
recovery/reconciliation in progress
authority continuity unverified
write-capable action restricted
read-only historical inspection may remain available
post-restore-point history may be incomplete
surviving material/effects observed but not automatically authoritative
```

Recovery therefore does not require a new Recovery concept or universal recovery lifecycle.

## Degraded-operation result

010-G rejects a global `degraded=true` semantic state.

The experience remains capability-specific:

- canonical persistence unavailable;
- projection/search unavailable;
- optional telemetry unavailable;
- exact dependency/source unavailable;
- output/checkpoint storage unavailable;
- compatible worker/accelerator unavailable;
- authorization/security indeterminate.

Each condition preserves its actual effect on actionability and authority without rewriting the committed semantic contract.

## History and Evidence result

Current and historical truth remain simultaneously inspectable.

History quality remains distinguishable as:

```text
directly retained
reconstructed
partial / incomplete
unavailable
indeterminate
```

Later Evidence invalidation/staleness/current inapplicability does not rewrite the historical Evaluation or a historical Generation completion that legitimately relied on that Evidence at the time. Current reliance must nevertheless expose the current Evidence applicability/status separately.

## Topology and text-bearing result

Time-series and multi-table summaries remain ergonomic views over exact Data Meaning structural semantics, Generation scope, Strategy capability, Constraint handling and Evaluation subjects.

Partial constituent progress does not establish whole Generation completion.

Text-bearing structured-data interaction preserves:

- Data Meaning text-role semantics;
- Strategy capability/limitations;
- direct vs learned behavior;
- dependency/network/runtime-distribution posture;
- text-scale limitations;
- relevant disclosure/memorization Evidence scope.

No Text, Relationship, Topology or Dataset concept is required by mapping.

## Enterprise-scale result

Ordinary human/programmatic inspection remains bounded/reference-first.

Neither surface requires the complete source/output/Learned-State/Evidence support/Provenance/log/telemetry corpus in one local process to determine ordinary semantic state.

Useful bounded interaction may expose logical identities, summaries, counts/scoped progress, material blockers, Evidence summaries, exact bindings and distributed/host diagnostic references.

The audit therefore preserves the design center where source/generated material may exceed driver or single-process memory.

## Actor result

### Platform Operator

Host-native operational detail may be richer than ordinary practitioner interaction, but platform job state remains operational evidence/correlation rather than domain completion authority.

### Extension Author / Library Maintainer

Extension capability and limitation declarations remain visible human/programmatically without allowing an implementation/plugin to redefine framework-wide concepts. Direct/learned mode, topology support, dependencies, network posture, resource requirements and approximation/claim limitations remain explicit where material.

No Operator State, Extension, Plugin or Capability concept is required.
