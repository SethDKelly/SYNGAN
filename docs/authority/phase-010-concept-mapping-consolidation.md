**State: CURRENTLY CLOSED.**

All 66 normalized command groups are mapped to actor intent and surface-neutral interaction obligations.

The mapping preserves that:

- conceptual actions are not automatically user controls;
- actor-triggered actions may require several later physical gestures;
- synchronization relevance does not create synchronization-owned controls;
- semantic commitment remains distinct from operational start;
- cancellation request remains distinct from terminal cancellation;
- result establishment remains owned by the relevant concept;
- contextual validation/readiness remains owned by consuming activities rather than a global Validation concept.

010-E through 010-G found no physical, family-composition or parity contradiction requiring F1 reopening.

# 5. F2 — state/query → inspection mapping

**State: CURRENTLY CLOSED.**

All 52 normalized query groups and all eleven lifecycle/history envelopes are mapped to actor-visible/programmatic inspection obligations.

The mapping preserves:

```text
current truth != historical/as-bound truth
semantic state != operational state
candidate material != authoritative result
Evidence finding != approval/release authority
Provenance relationship != referenced source fact
unknown != unavailable != withheld != absent

```

Inspection remains bounded/reference-first at enterprise scale. A combined view may compose facts but does not become a canonical owner.

010-G confirms the same distinctions survive recovery, partial history, withholding, scale and host degradation.

Controlling composition rule:

> **Concept inclusion defines available capability; it does not require every included concept to be re-executed in every invocation.**

Consequences include:

- an existing Learned State may be reused without new Learning;
- reusable Data Meaning, Strategy, Constraint and Criterion revisions may be selected rather than recreated;
- direct Generation is not a degraded learned workflow;
- Evaluation/Evidence are not universal Generation steps;
- Execution does not create an empty stage when absent;
- Provenance does not become a mandatory final gate;
- the full concept set does not become an eleven-stage wizard or Workflow concept.

Progressive disclosure remains semantic rather than UI architecture:

```text

D1  material semantic basis
D2  optional capability detail
D3  historical / explanatory depth
D4  distributed / host operational drill-down
```

# 8. F5 — human/programmatic semantic parity

**State: CURRENTLY CLOSED.**

Parity is defined as equivalent material semantics for the same authorized context and conceptual question/action, not identical ergonomics.

Human and programmatic surfaces preserve the material subset of:

- canonical owner and exact subject;
- semantic/current-use state;
- contextual assessment and actionability;
- Execution/Attempt state when present;
- candidate/finality distinction;
- exact bindings and temporal orientation;
- Evidence strength/uncertainty/limitations;
- Constraint applicability/handling;
- disclosure and history quality;
- authority-continuity qualification;
- bounded legitimate next-action semantics;
- distributed/host references where detail is not locally materialized.

The 20 difficult-condition probes pass across queueing, retry, cancellation ambiguity, unknown platform state, regressive recovery, persistence/projection/telemetry degradation, distributed runtime closure, partial storage material, protected existence, reconstructed history, later Evidence invalidation, evaluation-gated unfavorable Evidence, topology/text-bearing data, enterprise scale, approximation pressure, Platform Operator and extension-author interaction.

Parity is authorization-relative. Security policy may intentionally hide existence or collapse outward distinctions where disclosure itself is sensitive without mutating canonical truth.

# 12. Residual risks handed to Phase 011

These are **non-blocking design-quality risks**, not unresolved Phase 010 mapping gaps.

## R010-01 — composed specificity drift

The complete mapped product could still reveal that a concept purpose is too broad, too narrow or insufficiently distinct when judged as part of the final composed set rather than individually.

**Phase 011 obligation:** re-test specificity across the complete mapped concept family.

## R010-02 — familiarity versus semantic precision

Canonical terms are precise, but external Spark/data-science vocabulary such as model/run/job/artifact/metric may pressure later representations to use more familiar words in ways that collapse distinctions.

**Phase 011 obligation:** test whether the final vocabulary achieves familiarity without semantic substitution or unnecessary novelty.

## R010-03 — synchronization integrity under adversarial composition

010-F/G replay ordinary and difficult mappings, but broader adversarial histories may still reveal cross-concept pressure around correction, invalidation, evidence-gated completion, recovery and historical explanation.

**Phase 011 obligation:** adversarially test integrity and singular ownership across the final mapped synchronizations.

## R010-04 — synergy versus conceptual burden

The current eleven concepts compose without a hidden coordinator, but the overall mental model may still be more complex than necessary for some application-family members.

**Phase 011 obligation:** test whether each concept contributes enough distinct purpose to justify its compositional burden and whether simplification is possible without loss.

## R010-05 — progressive-disclosure misfit

D0-D4 keeps normal interaction concise, but an overly aggressive future representation could hide material limitations, Evidence strength, history quality or authority continuity.

**Phase 011 obligation:** test archetypal and exceptional scenarios for points where progressive disclosure itself could create conceptual misunderstanding.

## R010-06 — provider/host semantic leakage

Package-first mapping is host-agnostic across compliant Spark-capable platforms, but future Databricks/AWS/other adapters may tempt provider job, catalog, identity or artifact models to become canonical semantics.

**Phase 011 obligation:** use platform-specific counterexamples to test concept integrity without selecting adapter architecture.

## R010-07 — future capability pressure

Future Strategies, richer topology shapes, formal privacy mechanisms, advanced text capabilities or external integrations may introduce genuinely independent purposes/lifecycles not present now.

**Phase 011 obligation:** test extensibility/future-scope misfit and identify explicit concept-rediscovery triggers rather than pre-adding generic concepts.

## R010-08 — scale/approximation pressure

Enterprise resource pressure can tempt implementations to weaken Generation scope, Evaluation method, Evidence strength or distributed-runtime requirements while presenting a familiar successful experience.

**Phase 011 obligation:** continue adversarial scale/degradation testing at the concept-quality level, preserving explicit semantic ownership of approximation and limitation.

None of R010-01 through R010-08 blocks Phase 011 entry.
